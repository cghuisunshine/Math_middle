Lesson 17

Hockey-Stick Identity

In this lesson, I will explain each idea, read every expression aloud, and then guide you through the practice answer.

Add a diagonal line of combinations in Pascal’s Triangle.

Here is the main result.

Identity.

Read the expression as: r choose r plus r plus 1 choose r plus and so on plus n choose r equals n plus 1 choose r plus 1.

Now let us see why this works.

Combinatorial proof.

Count ways to choose r plus 1 numbers from 1,2, and so on ,n plus 1 . If the largest chosen number is k plus 1 , then the other r numbers must be chosen from 1,2, and so on ,k , giving k choose r choices. Summing over possible largest numbers gives the left side. Direct counting gives the right side.

Now let us work through an example.

Worked example.

Read the expression as: 2 choose 2 plus 3 choose 2 plus 4 choose 2 plus 5 choose 2 equals 1 plus 3 plus 6 plus 10 equals 20.

Read the expression as: 6 choose 3 equals 20.

Here is an important point to watch for.

Common mistake.

The lower number r stays fixed on the left. The upper number increases.

Now pause and try this practice problem yourself.

Practice.
