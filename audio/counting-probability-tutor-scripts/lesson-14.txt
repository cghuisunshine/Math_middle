Lesson 14

Dependent Probability

In this lesson, I will explain each idea, read every expression aloud, and then guide you through the practice answer.

When choices change the situation, update the sample space.

Let us start with the key idea.

Key idea.

Read the expression as: P open parenthesis A and B close parenthesis equals P open parenthesis A close parenthesis P open parenthesis B given A close parenthesis.

Now let us see why this works.

Why it works.

Among all outcomes, first restrict to the fraction where A happens. Inside that smaller world, multiply by the fraction where B also happens.

Now let us work through an example.

Worked example.

A bag has 5 red balls and 3 blue balls. Two balls are drawn without replacement. What is the probability both are red?

Read the expression as: the fraction 5 over 8 times the fraction 4 over 7 equals the fraction 20 over 56 equals the fraction 5 over 14.

Here is an important point to watch for.

Common mistake.

Without replacement means the denominator changes. With replacement, it would be the fraction 5 over 8 times the fraction 5 over 8 .

Now pause and try this practice problem yourself.

Practice.

A box has 4 white marbles and 6 black marbles. Two marbles are drawn without replacement. What is the probability both are black?

Let us check the answer.

Read the expression as: the fraction 6 over 10 times the fraction 5 over 9 equals the fraction 1 over 3.
