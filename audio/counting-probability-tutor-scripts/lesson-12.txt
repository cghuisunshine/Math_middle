Basic Probability

In this lesson, I will explain each idea, read every expression aloud, and then guide you through the practice answer.

Convert counting into probability when outcomes are equally likely.

Let us start with the key idea.

Key idea.

Read the expression as: P open parenthesis E close parenthesis equals the fraction favorable outcomes over total outcomes.

This formula requires equally likely outcomes.

Now let us see why this works.

Why it works.

If there are N equally likely outcomes, each has probability 1 divided by N . If event E contains k of them, then P open parenthesis E close parenthesis equals k times the fraction 1 over N equals the fraction k over N .

Now let us work through an example.

Worked example.

A fair die is rolled. What is the probability of rolling an even number?

Even outcomes are 2,4,6 , so:

Read the expression as: P open parenthesis even close parenthesis equals the fraction 3 over 6 equals the fraction 1 over 2.

Here is an important point to watch for.

Common mistake.

Do not use this simple fraction if the outcomes are not equally likely. For example, “rain” and “no rain” are not automatically 50-50.

Practice.
