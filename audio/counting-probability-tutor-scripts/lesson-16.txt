Lesson 16

Pascal’s Triangle

In this lesson, I will explain each idea, read every expression aloud, and then guide you through the practice answer.

See combinations in a triangle of numbers.

Let us start with the key idea.

Key idea.

Row n of Pascal’s Triangle contains:

Read the expression as: n choose 0,n choose 1,n choose 2, and so on ,n choose n.

Now let us see why this works.

Pascal’s identity proof.

To choose r people from n , choose a special person Alex. Either Alex is included, so choose r minus 1 from the remaining n minus 1 , or Alex is not included, so choose r from the remaining n minus 1 .

Read the expression as: n choose r equals n minus 1 choose r minus 1 plus n minus 1 choose r.

Let us use the visual to make the idea concrete.

Visual.

The displayed rows of Pascal's Triangle are: 1; 11; 121; 1331; 14641; 15101051.

Now let us work through an example.

Worked example.

Row 4 is 1,4,6,4,1 , so 4 choose 2 equals 6 .

Now pause and try this practice problem yourself.

Practice.

Use Pascal’s identity to compute 7 choose 3 from row 6.

Let us check the answer.

Read the expression as: 7 choose 3 equals 6 choose 2 plus 6 choose 3 equals 15 plus 20 equals 35.
