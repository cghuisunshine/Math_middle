Lesson 2

Addition, Subtraction, and Venn Diagrams

In this lesson, I will explain each idea, read every expression aloud, and then guide you through the practice answer.

Use addition for non-overlapping cases and subtraction to correct double counting.

Let us start with the key idea.

Key idea.

For two sets:

Read the expression as: the size of A union B equals the size of A plus the size of B minus the size of A intersection B.

Now let us see why this works.

Proof.

When we add the size of A plus the size of B , every item in the overlap A intersection B is counted twice. It should be counted once, so subtract the overlap once.

Now let us work through an example.

Worked example.

A class has 30 students. 18 take French, 14 take Spanish, and 7 take both. How many take French or Spanish?

Read the expression as: 18 plus 14 minus 7 equals 25.

Let us use the visual to make the idea concrete.

Visual.

The visual shows: Venn diagram for French and Spanish.

The union is 11 plus 7 plus 7 equals 25 .

Now pause and try this practice problem yourself.

Practice.

There are 40 students. 22 play soccer, 18 play basketball, and 9 play both. How many play at least one of the two sports?

Let us check the answer.

Read the expression as: 22 plus 18 minus 9 equals 31.

That completes lesson 2. Review the key idea, then try the practice problem once more without looking at the answer.
